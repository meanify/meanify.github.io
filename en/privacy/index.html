Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Meanify, ÉvoraTech, Évora, Portugal, which is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at info@meanify.eu or +351 962 390 920.

2. Information we collect

When you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details and the content of your message. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics also collects usage data such as the pages you visit, your device and browser type, your approximate location and the website that referred you.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to reply to your enquiries, provide our services, improve this website and meet our legal obligations. We reply to enquiries on the basis of our legitimate interest and to take steps before entering into a contract with you. We use analytics only with your consent. We do not sell or rent your personal data.

4. Who we share it with

Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited) processes usage data on our behalf, only after you accept analytics cookies. If you contact us on WhatsApp, your messages are also processed by WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited) under its own privacy policy. We do not share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and our business relationship, and as required by law. Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months.

6. Cookies

Details of the cookies and browser storage we use, and how to change your choice, are in our Cookie Policy.

7. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Portuguese law, you can access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, object to processing, and ask for a copy of your data. You can withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email info@meanify.eu. You also have the right to complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (www.cnpd.pt).

8. Contact

For any questions about this policy or your personal data, contact us at info@meanify.eu or call +351 962 390 920. Our address is ÉvoraTech, Évora, Portugal.
