AI-Accelerated Development

AI-accelerated development is how Meanify builds software. Our Forward Deployed Engineers work inside your team and use proven AI workflows to write code, tests and documentation faster. An engineer reviews every change before it ships, so you get speed and lower cost without losing quality or control.

How it works

  1. 1

    Discovery

    A short workshop to agree on scope, priorities and what success looks like.

    Human checkpoint: You approve the scope.

  2. 2

    Set-up

    Engineers join your team and set up the AI-assisted workflow in your repositories.

    Human checkpoint: Your team agrees on tools and access.

  3. 3

    Build in short iterations

  4. 4

Frequently asked questions

What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

A Forward Deployed Engineer is a software engineer who works directly inside a client's team, close to the users and the problem, instead of building in isolation. At Meanify, our Forward Deployed Engineers also bring validated AI workflows that speed up delivery.

Is AI-written code safe to use in production?

Only when it is reviewed and tested. At Meanify, an engineer reviews every AI-assisted change, and it must pass automated tests before it is merged. AI speeds up the work; engineers stay responsible for the result.

Why is AI-accelerated development cheaper?

AI handles a large share of repetitive work such as boilerplate code, tests and documentation, so engineers spend their time on design, review and the hard problems. Fewer hours are needed to reach the same quality.

Who owns the code?

You do. The code is written in your repositories and handed over with documentation so your team can maintain it.
