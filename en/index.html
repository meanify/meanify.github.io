How we work

A validated process, with a human in the loop

The same five steps on every project. AI speeds up the work; people make the decisions.

  1. 1

    Discover

    We learn your process, goals and constraints, and agree on what success looks like.

    Human checkpoint: You approve the scope and success criteria.

  2. 2

    Plan

    We define the architecture, the iterations and where the review checkpoints are.

    Human checkpoint: You approve the plan.

  3. 3

    Build with AI

    Engineers use validated AI workflows to draft code, tests, automations and documentation.

    Human checkpoint: Engineers design and guide the work.

  4. 4

    Validate

    Every change is reviewed by an engineer and checked by automated tests before it is merged.

    Human checkpoint: Nothing ships without review.

  5. 5

    Deliver and support

    We demo, deploy and hand over with documentation, then support you after go-live.

    Human checkpoint: You accept each delivery.

FAQ

Common questions

What does Meanify do?

Meanify is an AI engineering company based in Évora, Portugal. Our Forward Deployed Engineers use AI-accelerated development to build software, AI automation, multi-agent systems, data pipelines and governance, and small physical AI projects, with a human reviewing every step.

What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

A Forward Deployed Engineer is a software engineer who works directly inside a client's team, close to the users and the problem, instead of building in isolation. At Meanify, our Forward Deployed Engineers also bring validated AI workflows that speed up delivery.

What does "human in the loop" mean?

It means a person reviews and approves the work at defined points. At Meanify, an engineer reviews every AI-assisted change, automated tests check it, and you approve every delivery.

Is our data used to train AI models?

No. We use AI services and settings that do not train on client data, and we can use EU-hosted or on-premise models when required.
